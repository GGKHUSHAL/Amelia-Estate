Project Overview

Amelia Estate II is a luxurious, low-density residential project featuring exclusive Stilt+4 floor 3BHK apartments. With only 4 homes per floor, every residence ensures premium privacy and unobstructed views.

Key Highlights

  • MIVAN construction technology for superior quality
  • Vastu-compliant design for optimal living
  • Earthquake-resistant structure with advanced safety features
  • Premium brand specifications (Kohler, Grohe, Godrej)
  • Spacious balconies with city views
  • Multi-level parking and stilt-floor planning

Lifestyle Amenities

Residents enjoy access to an infinity pool, state-of-the-art fitness studio, clubhouse, landscaped gardens, kids play zone, and 24×7 security with CCTV monitoring.

Location Advantage

Just 15 minutes from Chandigarh International Airport, near PR-7 Airport Road and Aero City. Close to premium schools, hospitals, retail, and daily conveniences making it ideal for families.

Investment Highlights
